# IEEE TIM Venue Profile

Use this profile as general guidance for IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation and Measurement-style manuscripts. Do not include project-specific claims here.

## Good Fit

- Measurement systems, instrumentation methods, calibration, diagnostics, uncertainty, characterization, and validation.
- Papers where the central contribution is how a phenomenon is measured, diagnosed, quantified, or made reproducible.
- Hardware or embedded systems work with strong experimental methodology and traceable evidence.

## Writing Emphasis

- Lead with the measurement problem and why existing measurements are insufficient.
- Explain the instrumentation or diagnostic method clearly.
- Report setup, sample sizes, uncertainty sources, repeatability, and limitations.
- Make figures and tables serve measurement interpretation.
- Keep claims tied to observed data and measurement boundaries.

## Common Risks

- Framing the work as only an implementation artifact without a measurement contribution.
- Underexplaining calibration, repeatability, or uncertainty.
- Overclaiming generality beyond the tested setup.
- Treating standards-related evidence as certification without a complete validation package.

## Revision Focus

- Strengthen experimental design and measurement traceability.
- Add comparison between measurement conditions, not only final performance numbers.
- Move unsupported mechanism claims into discussion or limitations.
