# IEEE Access Venue Profile

Use this profile as general guidance for IEEE Access-style manuscripts. Do not include project-specific claims here.

## Good Fit

- Broad engineering studies with complete workflows, interdisciplinary relevance, and practical reproducibility.
- Papers that combine design, implementation, evaluation, and open or inspectable artifacts.
- Work that may not fit a narrow transactions scope but has clear technical value.

## Writing Emphasis

- State the practical problem and broad relevance early.
- Present a coherent system or workflow, not disconnected experiments.
- Make reproducibility, artifacts, and engineering decisions easy to inspect.
- Keep limitations explicit and concrete.
- Use accessible technical prose while preserving rigorous evidence boundaries.

## Common Risks

- Becoming too broad or survey-like.
- Losing a single central thesis.
- Overloading the manuscript with implementation detail that does not support claims.
- Using weak comparisons or unsupported generalization.

## Revision Focus

- Improve narrative coherence and artifact traceability.
- Consolidate secondary results into tables or appendices.
- Make the contribution legible to adjacent fields.
