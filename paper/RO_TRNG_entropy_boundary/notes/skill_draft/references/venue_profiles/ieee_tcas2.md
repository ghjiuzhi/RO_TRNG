# IEEE TCAS-II Venue Profile

Use this profile as general guidance for IEEE Transactions on Circuits and Systems II-style short manuscripts. Do not include project-specific claims here.

## Good Fit

- Concise circuits-and-systems contributions with a sharp technical insight.
- Brief papers where one method, circuit behavior, diagnostic, or design implication can be stated and supported compactly.
- Work that can be understood through a small number of figures, equations, or experiments.

## Writing Emphasis

- Make the central contribution narrow and unmistakable.
- Cut background, workflow detail, and secondary experiments aggressively.
- Prioritize circuit/system mechanism, design implication, and compact validation.
- Use dense but readable figures and tables.

## Common Risks

- Trying to fit a broad experimental campaign into a short-paper format.
- Presenting engineering workflow instead of a crisp circuits-and-systems result.
- Depending on too many claims or too many experimental branches.

## Revision Focus

- Reduce the manuscript to one primary message.
- Keep only evidence that directly supports that message.
- Move broad reproducibility or exploratory analysis out of scope.
