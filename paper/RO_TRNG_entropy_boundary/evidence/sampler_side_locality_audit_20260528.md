# Sampler-Side Locality Audit 20260528

This audit records the current implementation evidence for the sampler-side counterfactual claim. It is intended to answer the reviewer question: if FIFO, control, and UART cells also move in routed diffs, why is the result still interpreted as sampler-side boundary evidence?

## Short Answer

The current evidence does not prove that only the sample RO changed, and the manuscript should not claim that. The defensible claim is narrower:

> In the same-RTL forward counterfactual, the intended constraint perturbation is the sample-RO LOC/BEL lock. At the cell-placement level, tracked data-RO cells and sampled-data registers remain fixed, while sample-RO cells change. At the route level, Vivado still changes some data-RO and sample-RO nets, so the result supports sampler-side physical implementation plus local routed context as the measured boundary factor, not sample-RO LUT delay alone.

FIFO, control, and UART movement is treated as a residual implementation confounder and measurement-chain traceability issue. It is not treated as evidence that the whole bitstream belongs inside the entropy-source boundary.

## RTL Readout Feedback Audit

The entropy bit is generated inside `entropy_source.v`:

- data ROs drive `sampled_data`;
- sample-RO phases clock the sampled-data registers;
- `rand_bit <= ^sampled_data` is clocked by the sample RO;
- `clk_o` is driven from the final sample-RO stage.

The restart tops instantiate `entropy_source`, then write `rand_bit` into an asynchronous FIFO using `rand_clk`. UART logic reads the FIFO on the 200 MHz system clock side.

Important boundary observations:

- FIFO and UART do not feed a data value back into `sampled_data` or `rand_bit`.
- `tx_ready` controls FIFO read/send progress, not the combinational or register-level construction of `rand_bit`.
- `fifo_full` gates FIFO write enable (`fifo_wr_en = ro_en & ~fifo_full`) but does not directly gate `ro_en` or the sample-RO/data-RO logic inside `entropy_source`.
- `fifo_empty`, `tx_ready`, and the top-level FSM still define the measurement protocol: drain, warmup, send timing, and which generated bytes are transmitted or discarded. Therefore readout/control placement is outside the entropy source but remains a measurement-chain confounder that must be reported.

This is why the paper should not move FIFO/UART inside the entropy-source boundary. The better wording is that FIFO/control/UART movement remains a residual routed-neighborhood or protocol confounder, while the positive boundary evidence comes from the sampler-side counterfactual and entropy-source-local implementation audit.

## Routed Locality Results

### Forward W4: compact diagnostic with Scompact vs Srestart

This is the strongest locality comparison because it keeps the compact diagnostic RTL configuration and compares the compact sample-RO lock against the restart-oriented sample-RO lock.

Source files:

- `data/experiments/sample_ro_route_diff_20260528/compact_w4_baseline_vs_forward_w4_formal_sample_cell_diff_20260528.csv`
- `data/experiments/sample_ro_route_diff_20260528/compact_w4_baseline_vs_forward_w4_formal_sample_net_diff_20260528.csv`

Cell-level result:

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| data_ro | 16 | 0 | 0 |
| sampled_data_regs | 64 | 0 | 0 |
| sample_ro | 9 | 2 | 3 |

Route-level result:

| Net group | Common nets | Routes changed |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| data_ro_net | 64 | 34 |
| sample_ro_net | 36 | 18 |
| sampled_data_net | 64 | 3 |

Net-delay summary from extracted Vivado `get_net_delays`:

| Net group | Slow max mean, Scompact | Slow max mean, Srestart | Delta |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |

Interpretation:

- The positive locality evidence is cell-level: tracked data-RO cells and sampled-data registers stay fixed, while sample-RO LOC/BEL assignments change.
- The route-level evidence is not isolated: data-RO and sample-RO internal routes both change, although sampled-data register input net delays are nearly unchanged by the mean summary.
- Therefore the claim should be "sampler-side physical implementation and local routed context", not "only sample-RO LUT delay".

- The route residual remains nonzero, so the conclusion stays bounded to sampler-side physical implementation and local routed context rather than sample-RO-only causality.

- It supports the bidirectional diagnostic story, but should not be used as proof that only the sample RO changed.

## What This Adds To The Paper

The audit strengthens the paper in three ways:

1. It separates the strongest physical-locality evidence from the broader reverse repair evidence.
2. It explains why FIFO/UART movement should be reported as a measurement-chain confounder, not promoted into the entropy-source boundary.
3. It gives reviewers concrete routed-cell, routed-net, and net-delay evidence instead of only saying that such evidence is future work.

## Remaining Evidence Gap

The next stronger experiment would be a route-locked repeat that holds data-RO internal routes and post-XOR readout/control placement more invariant while perturbing the sample-RO placement. Until then, the paper should keep the attribution bounded to the measured sampler-side physical implementation and local routed context.
