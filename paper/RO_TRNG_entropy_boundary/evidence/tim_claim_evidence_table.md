## Use Rules

- Upgrade no claim beyond the cited local evidence.
- Keep abstract and conclusion claims inside the single-project evidence boundary.
- Use `measurement-driven characterization`, `TDC-assisted diagnosis`, and `entropy-source boundary` as the TIM vocabulary.
- State SP800-90B material as entropy-assessment or restart evidence, not certification.
